# Mochi's Nap Room 🐱

A tiny, cozy, no-stress cat game. No timers, no losing — just a cat.

Open `index.html` in any browser (desktop or phone). That's it.

- ✋ **Pet** — stroke Mochi for purrs and hearts, or tap the floor to call them over
- 🪶 **Play** — wiggle the feather wand and Mochi will chase and pounce
- 🐟 **Treat** — tap the floor to drop a fishy snack
- 🎨 change fur · 🎀 wear unlocked accessories · 🔊 sound on/off

Leave Mochi alone for a bit and they'll wander off to their bed for a nap.
Hearts unlock a bow, a bell collar, a flower crown, a party hat and a tiny crown.
Progress is saved in your browser.
